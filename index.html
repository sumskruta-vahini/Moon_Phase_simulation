    How to explore the sky

    A quick tour of the controls before you enter the simulation.

    • ⚲
      Search

      Type a name in the sidebar to filter the constellation list.

    • ☉
      Select

      Click a constellation in the list — or a star on the chart — to focus it.

    • ⊕
      Adaptive zoom

      The chart automatically frames the selected constellation so it fills the view.

    • ⓘ
      Details panel

      Read mythology, key facts, and a sortable table of the constellation's stars.

    • ⎆
      Keyboard

      Use ↑/↓ to move through the list and Enter to select.
